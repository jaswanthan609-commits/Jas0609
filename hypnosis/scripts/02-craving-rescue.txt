# Session 2: Craving rescue. About five minutes, eyes open or closed, for use in the middle of a craving.
# Urge surfing plus slow exhale breathing. Long exhales slow the heart (resonance breathing, about six breaths a minute).
@title Ride the Wave
@subtitle Five minutes for a craving
@short

You feel a craving. That's alright.
You don't have to do anything about it except breathe.
[pause 3]
Press your thumb and first finger together.
Breathe in, gently, for a count of four.
[pause 4]
And out, slowly, for a count of six.
[pause 6]
Again. In.
[pause 4]
And out, long and slow.
[pause 6]

Now notice where the craving lives in your body.
The chest. The throat. The mouth. The hands.
Just notice it. You don't have to push it away.
[pause 6]
Give it a number from one to ten.
How strong is it, right now?
[pause 6]

A craving is a wave.
It rises, it peaks, and it falls. Every single time.
Your job is just to stay on the board.
[pause 4]
Breathe in.
[pause 4]
Breathe out.
[pause 6]
Watch the wave. Is it rising, or already at the top?
[pause 8]
Breathe in.
[pause 4]
And out.
[pause 6]
Notice any small change. A little looser, a little softer. Or simply the same. Both are fine.
[pause 8]

Remember your most important reason for being free.
Say it to yourself, quietly, in your own words.
[pause 8]
Picture the people or things that matter most to you.
This moment is you protecting them.
[pause 6]

Breathe in.
[pause 4]
And out.
[pause 6]
Check the number again. One to ten.
[pause 6]
Most waves have already started falling by now.
If it's still there, that's fine. Keep breathing with me.
[pause 3]
In.
[pause 4]
Out.
[pause 6]
In.
[pause 4]
Out.
[pause 6]

Now do one small thing with your hands or your body.
Drink some water. Stand up and stretch. Walk to another room.
Give the wave nowhere to land.
[pause 4]
You rode this one.
You'll ride the next one, and each one gets a little smaller.
Release your fingers. Well done.
[pause 4]
