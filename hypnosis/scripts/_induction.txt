# Shared induction: eye fixation, slow-exhale breathing (about six breaths a minute), progressive relaxation, counted deepening.
# Format: one sentence per line. A blank line is a short rest. [pause N] is N seconds of quiet.

Find a position where your body can be fully supported.
Sitting or lying down, whatever feels easiest right now.
[pause 3]
If you like, let your eyes rest on the soft circle in front of you.
Watch it slowly grow, and slowly settle.
[pause 4]
As it grows, breathe in gently through your nose.
As it settles, let the breath flow out, slow and long.
[pause 8]
In, as the circle grows.
[pause 4]
And out, a little slower.
[pause 6]
Again. Breathing in.
[pause 4]
And letting go.
[pause 6]

There is nothing you need to do well here.
Just follow along, in your own way.
[pause 3]
Your eyelids may begin to feel a little heavy.
Whenever they want to close, let them close.
[pause 6]

Now bring your attention to your forehead.
Let the small muscles there soften.
[pause 4]
Let that softness move down around your eyes.
Your jaw can loosen, and your tongue can rest.
[pause 5]
Your shoulders can drop, just a little, on the next out breath.
[pause 6]
Feel your arms becoming heavy and warm.
All the way down to your fingertips.
[pause 5]
Your chest rises and falls on its own.
Your stomach is soft.
[pause 5]
Your legs grow heavy, and your feet rest on the ground, or on the bed.
[pause 6]
The whole body is supported.
You don't have to hold anything up anymore.
[pause 6]

In a moment I'll count down from five to one.
With each number, you can let yourself drift twice as deep.

[pause 5]

Two.
Calm, and safe, and settled.
