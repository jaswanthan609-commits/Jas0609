# Session 3: Courage. Use when there's something you've been avoiding: a conversation, a call, a decision, a new step.
# Techniques: hypnotic relaxation for anxiety (Valentine 2019), recalling past mastery (Bandura's self-efficacy),
# step-by-step mental rehearsal (imagery exposure and mental practice), instructional self-talk, if-then plans.
@title Steady Courage
@subtitle A self-hypnosis session for acting despite fear
@include _induction

Courage isn't the absence of fear.
It's moving toward what matters while the fear comes along for the ride.
[pause 6]

Let your mind drift back to a time when you were scared, and you did it anyway.
It might be big or small.
A first day somewhere new. Speaking up. Asking for something. Starting over after a setback.
[pause 10]
Let that memory become clearer.
Where were you? Who was there?
[pause 6]
Remember how it felt just before. The nerves. The doubt.
[pause 5]
And then remember that you went ahead.
[pause 4]
Feel what that was like, afterwards.
The relief. The quiet strength of knowing you did it.
[pause 8]
That strength didn't go anywhere.
It's part of you, right now.
[pause 6]

Now press your thumb and first finger together, and breathe out slowly.
Let the feeling from that memory flow into this anchor.
Steady. Grounded. Capable.
[pause 8]
Release.
[pause 3]

Now bring to mind the thing you've been putting off.
Just see it from a distance at first, like a picture across the room.
[pause 6]
Notice any tension in your body as you look at it.
Breathe into that tension. Long, slow breath out.
[pause 8]
Now let the picture come a little closer.
And breathe again, letting the body stay soft.
[pause 8]

Now imagine the very first step. Only the first one.
Maybe it's opening the message. Picking up the phone. Walking through the door.
[pause 5]
See yourself doing that first step.
Notice the nerves, and notice that you keep going anyway.
Your feet move. Your voice works. Your hands do what they need to.
[pause 10]
Say to yourself, calmly, one step at a time.
[pause 4]
Breathe. Next step.
[pause 4]
See yourself take the next step. And the next.
[pause 10]
Things might not go perfectly in your picture, and that's fine.
See yourself handling whatever happens, breathing, staying steady, and carrying on.
[pause 10]
Now see yourself finished.
It's done. You did it.
Feel that same relief and strength from your memory, here now as well.
[pause 10]

Let's set a plan.
When the moment comes and I feel fear rising, then I press my thumb and finger, breathe out slowly, and take only the first step.
[pause 6]
See that plan running smoothly, on its own.
[pause 6]

Each time you act despite fear, the fear gets a little quieter, and you get a little stronger.
That's how courage grows. One step at a time.
[pause 4]
You've done hard things before.
[pause 3]
You can do the next one.
[pause 8]
