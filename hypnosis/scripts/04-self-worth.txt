# Session 4: Self-worth and confidence.
# Techniques: values self-affirmation (Epton 2015; Cohen and Sherman 2014), compassionate-friend imagery (self-compassion,
# Ferrari 2019 meta-analysis), evidence-based recall of real strengths, Best Possible Self imagery (Carrillo 2019 meta-analysis).
# Deliberately avoids big "I am amazing" affirmations: those made people with low self-esteem feel worse (Wood, Perunovic and Lee 2009).
# Uses evidence from your own life and kind self-talk instead.
@title Quiet Confidence
@subtitle A self-hypnosis session for self-worth
@include _induction

In this calm place, there's nothing you need to prove.
You can simply be here, just as you are.
[pause 6]

Think of what you care about most.
Maybe honesty. Loyalty. Family. Learning. Hard work. Kindness. Humour.
[pause 6]
Choose one value that feels most like you.
[pause 5]
Now remember a time you lived that value.
A moment when you acted on it, even when it wasn't easy.
[pause 10]
See that moment clearly.
That was you. That is you.
Your worth shows up in moments like these, not in what anyone else says about you.
[pause 8]

Now think of a few things you have actually done.
Things you got through. Skills you learned. People you helped.
Not what you wish you'd done. What you really did.
[pause 10]
Let each one rise up and be counted.
One.
[pause 5]
Another.
[pause 5]
And another.
[pause 6]
These are facts. They belong to you.
[pause 6]

Now imagine a good friend sitting beside you.
Someone kind, who knows you well, and wants the best for you.
[pause 6]
Imagine you've had a hard day. Maybe you made a mistake, or you feel you've fallen short.
[pause 5]
Listen to what that friend says to you.
Not criticism. Not empty praise. Just warmth and truth.
Perhaps something like, you're doing your best, and that's enough for today.
Or, everyone stumbles. It doesn't change who you are.
[pause 10]
Notice how it feels to be spoken to that way.
[pause 6]
Now, try saying those same words to yourself.
In your own mind, in a kind voice.
[pause 10]
That voice is yours. You can use it any time.
Especially when the old critical voice gets loud.
[pause 6]

Press your thumb and first finger together, and breathe out slowly.
Let that warmth and steadiness link to this anchor.
[pause 8]
Release.
[pause 3]

Now imagine yourself one year from now.
Everything has gone about as well as it realistically could.
You've worked hard, and it's paying off.
[pause 6]
See how you carry yourself.
See how you speak to people. Calm, direct, unhurried.
You look people in the eye. You say what you think, kindly.
[pause 10]
Notice how you treat yourself when things go wrong.
You don't tear yourself down. You learn, and you keep going.
[pause 8]
That future you didn't become someone new.
They just kept choosing to act on their values, and to speak to themselves with respect.
[pause 6]
You can start that today.
[pause 6]

Confidence isn't feeling sure about everything.
It's trusting that you can handle what comes, and that you're worth taking care of.
[pause 5]
You've handled hard things before.
[pause 3]
You have values you live by.
[pause 3]
And you deserve the same kindness you would give a friend.
[pause 8]
@include _emergence
